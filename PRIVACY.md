# Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026.

This policy covers the Kiln website at [usekiln.pages.dev](https://usekiln.pages.dev), the `@electro1207/kiln` npm package, the component registry and the `kiln-add.mjs` installer. Kiln is an open-source project run by its contributors on [GitHub](https://github.com/Electro1207/Kiln).

## In short

Kiln has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information. A few preferences are kept in your own browser, and two outside services (Cloudflare and Google Fonts) see the basic request details any website visit sends.

## What stays in your browser

The website keeps these settings in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and you can clear them at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.

- `kiln-theme`: light, dark or follow the system.
- `kiln-nav-open`: which groups of the sidebar you left open.
- `kiln-studio`: the theme you are building in the theme studio.
- `kiln-spotlight-recent`: recent searches typed into the Spotlight search demo.

## Demos

Every demo runs entirely in your browser. Text you type into a demo form (sign-in, checkout, contact, comments, chat and the rest), files you drop or crop, signatures you draw and colours you pick with the eyedropper are never sent anywhere. Nothing a demo shows as "sent" or "saved" leaves your device.

## Services that see your visit

- **Cloudflare Pages** hosts the website, the registry and the installer. To serve and protect them, Cloudflare processes your IP address, browser details and the pages you request, as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We do not use Cloudflare's analytics.
- **Google Fonts** serves the IBM Plex typefaces. Your browser asks Google for the font files, which shows Google your IP address and browser details. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub** sees your visit only when you follow a link to it, for example to report a bug or request a feature. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **npm** handles your download when you install the package. See [npm's privacy policy](https://docs.npmjs.com/policies/privacy).

## The library and installer

The npm package and the components copied from the registry contain no telemetry and make no network requests of their own. A few components let your app load media you point them at, such as a video or image URL; those requests go only where you send them. The installer downloads component files from the Kiln website and nothing else.

## Children

Kiln is a tool for developers and is not aimed at children. Because we collect no personal information, we hold none about children either.

## Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. For data that Cloudflare, Google, GitHub or npm process, use the controls and contacts in their policies above.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. Earlier versions stay in the repository's history.

## Contact

Ask a question in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Electro1207/Kiln/discussions) or [open an issue](https://github.com/Electro1207/Kiln/issues). For anything sensitive, use private vulnerability reporting under **Security** on the repository, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
